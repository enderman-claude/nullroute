You send the audit trail, the routing export and the fleet log to three
newsrooms and a transport regulator, with the manifest attached.

By the next morning the story is everywhere. Meridian suspends the board.
Vesna Halden is stopped at a train station, still holding a coffee. The
insulin reached the hospital forty minutes late. Nobody can make that up.

Your name is not in any of the reports. You go back to the rig and wait
for the next dead drop. It is only a matter of time.
