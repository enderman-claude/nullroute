# NULLROUTE: the story

It is 2041. Delivery drones carry most of the city's parcels, the ledgers that
pay for them are smart contracts, and a few thousand systems were connected
to each other without anyone deciding to. You play **ghost**, a freelance
operator running jobs from a rig above a noodle bar in the old harbour
district.

A job arrives through a dead drop, signed **V**. It starts with a bakery.

| Ch | Title         | Goal                                    | What you learn                                           |
|----|---------------|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | Cold Start    | Download the cash drawer log            | The bakery's point-of-sale syncs to a logistics gateway  |
| 2  | Dead Drop     | Download the routing export             | A hospital zone is rerouted to a residential one         |
| 3  | Ghost Route   | Take the fleet override key from root   | Couriers can be steered by a board vote                  |
| 4  | Quorum        | Pull the audit trail                    | The vote passed 5 to 3, approved by the board chair      |
| 5  | Null Route    | Read the broker's manifest              | V is the chair. You were the cover, not the client       |

At the end you choose:

- `publish`: send the evidence to the press. The board is suspended, the chair
  is arrested, and you go back to waiting for the next dead drop.
- `sell`: take the chair's money. The routes stay as they are, and the next
  dead drop is already on its way.

Both endings are short on purpose. The story is in what the player finds
along the way, in the logs and mail, not in long cut-scenes.
